What it adds up to

In 9 months of production work, 412 pull requests merged, adding 410K+ lines across three AI products and the infrastructure under them: a voice assistant with long-term memory, a computer-vision inventory product for insurance claims, and a multi-tenant platform that runs a private team of AI agents for every workspace.

The pattern repeats across all of it — measure before claiming (held-out evals, concurrency benchmarks), migrate in stages instead of big-bang cutovers, and treat idempotency and concurrency as product features.

197 commits are co-authored with AI coding agents: AI, built with AI.

  1. ARe-architected per-item pricing as a parallel retail-search fan-out with LLM ranking, projected in the PR at ~5s versus ~60s before.
  2. BValidated a vision fix on a held-out, human-labelled set: exact-count rate 81% → 83%, and over-counts per photo run fell 0.53 → 0.43.
  3. CAuth moved in stages, never a big-bang cutover: SimpleJWT to Firebase on one product, a phased move to Better Auth that kept row-level security on another, and a new registration control plane for a third.
  4. DRe-platformed a production AI product onto AWS EKS with Terraform, SQS workers and idempotent at-least-once processing.
  5. EGot a fix merged upstream into an open-source agent runtime.
